Initiate a New Task

System administrators / super-admins can initiate collection, processing or review tasks. You may assign specific members (manual assignment), publish to the open pool for eligible roles to claim, or let the system auto-balance the workload. Once published, the task enters the workflow and assignees receive an in-site notification.
